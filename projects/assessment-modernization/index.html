Project 06 • Assessment Operations

Assessment Workflow Modernization

Using automation for repetitive objective marking while preserving professional judgment for speaking, writing, and feedback.

The challenge

Multiple-choice examinations created a large volume of repetitive hand-marking. At the same time, students needed practice materials that reflected the structure and language demands of the assessment without reproducing live exam content.

The opportunity was not to automate assessment indiscriminately. It was to separate objective handling from work that requires a teacher’s interpretation, then improve each part of the process.

My contribution

I advocated for the use of ZipGrade and bubble-sheet responses for objective Pre-Intermediate examinations. I also provide feedback on exam formatting and develop review or revision lessons based on the assessment structure.

Clear role boundary

The programme coordinator is responsible for creating the examinations. My contribution is workflow improvement, formatting support, feedback, and the development of aligned revision materials.

Why the workflow matters

  • Teacher time is redirected: less repetitive objective marking, with more attention available for qualitative work.
  • Formatting becomes functional: clear layouts support scanning, administration, and student use.
  • Preparation stays aligned: review materials connect classroom practice to assessment expectations.
  • Automation remains selective: technology supports the process without replacing teacher judgment.

Current status

ZipGrade is currently used for objective examinations at the Pre-Intermediate level. Broader use across the programme has been discussed, but this case study does not present that expansion as completed.

Reflection

Assessment modernization does not require automating everything. A stronger design identifies which tasks are repetitive and rules-based, then protects teacher time for the work that depends on observation, interpretation, and meaningful feedback.

Privacy and assessment security

No student records, live exam questions, answer keys, or confidential results are displayed in this case study.
