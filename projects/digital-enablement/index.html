Project 07 • Faculty Support

Digital Enablement and Faculty Support

Helping colleagues use shared teaching systems in the devices and classrooms they actually have.

Challenge

The environment includes mixed hardware, classroom computers, personal devices, account restrictions, and unfamiliar authentication procedures. A technically useful system can fail if colleagues cannot access it.

Design response

I help colleagues use Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, configure classroom access, open teaching files, use Microsoft Authenticator, troubleshoot OneDrive, and work around device limitations.

How it works

  1. Identify the actual barrier: account, permission, device, software, or hardware.
  2. Configure the minimum working path.
  3. Test it on the device and classroom computer the colleague will use.
  4. Provide follow-up when authentication or syncing interrupts the workflow.
  5. Use recurring support problems to improve the system or instructions.

Reach and reflection

This informal support enables broader use of shared materials and systems such as centralized attendance. The project does not claim a formal IT or management title.

Evidence to addStaff onboarding checklist, sanitized SharePoint information architecture, and an access-troubleshooting flowchart.

Lesson learned

Design for the least convenient real environment, not the ideal demonstration environment. Technology adoption is often harder than technology creation.
