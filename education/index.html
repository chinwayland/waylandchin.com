Education & Innovation

Education works better when the system around the teacher works better.

I teach university English and develop the curriculum, assessment tools, shared systems, and technical workflows that make teaching more consistent and classroom-ready.

View projectsCV & documents

My approach

My work begins with direct observation: identify where a lesson or process creates friction, redesign it, use it in the real environment, and refine it based on what happens. Technology is useful when it removes repetitive work, improves consistency, or makes expectations clearer.

Teaching scope

  • More than seven years of experience in Chinese higher education.
  • Approximately 100–125 undergraduate students taught each semester.
  • Approximately 60 lessons delivered per semester across Elementary, Pre-Intermediate, and Intermediate levels.
  • Approximately 100–125 individual speaking assessments administered each semester.

Curriculum contribution

I co-developed the programme curriculum, created approximately five weeks of original lessons during the initial build, and now develop, review, standardize, and refine approximately 120 university lessons each academic year.

Lessons can include multiple instructional stages, student worksheets, teacher guides, answer keys, illustrations, and audio. The aim is to let an instructor walk into class and teach without first rebuilding the material.
